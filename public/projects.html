istheldown.com

A real-time map of every train in New York. A Cloudflare Worker and a Durable Object poll the MTA’s GTFS-Realtime feeds every 15 seconds and push the diffs over a WebSocket to a MapLibre map: subway, LIRR, Metro-North, NJ Transit, ferries, buses, and every service alert, with a page per line that answers “is the L down?”

TypeScript · Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects, R2 · MapLibre GL

mingus

A map of your music taste. It takes a Spotify extended streaming history export, tags every artist with genres from Last.fm and MusicBrainz, projects them into two dimensions with UMAP, and renders the result as a WebGL scatter with a time scrubber, so you can watch your taste drift over the life of the account. The pipeline runs on Modal.

Python, polars, UMAP · Modal · WebGL

xyz

This site. Rust compiled to WebAssembly and run on Cloudflare Workers through workers-rs. The pages are static files served from the edge; the only dynamic route is the random-cig button, which streams an image out of an R2 bucket.

Rust · workers-rs · Cloudflare Workers, R2

cig_scraper

Pulls all 9,998 Cigarette Pack NFT images from IPFS into an R2 bucket, fanned out across Modal containers in batches of a hundred with retries and resumable runs. That bucket is what the random-cig button on the homepage reads from.

Python · Modal · Cloudflare R2
